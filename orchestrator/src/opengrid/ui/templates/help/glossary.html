Glossary

Terms and abbreviations

Definitions match the r3.4 code. Use the page search to filter the rows.

TermMeaning in OpenGrid
ALR-*An alert rule id, for example ALR-FEED-STALE or ALR-RESERVE-BREACH. Open alerts are rows in og.alert and appear on the Health page; each rule belongs to one module that raises and clears it. See Alert rules.
AllocatorThe real-time dispatcher (opengrid.allocator) run by og-engine every 2 s. It turns commitments into per-bank grants, substitutes hubs within an obligation, runs the DIST_DEFERRAL PI loop, enforces flow limits and territory, and sells leftover headroom. It never selects new work and never moves committed capacity to another obligation.
AnchorThe trace chain's head hash, signed and written outside the database ([trace].anchor_dir and a secondary copy) and recorded in og.trace_anchor, so the chain can be checked even if the database were altered (K11). Failures raise ALR-ANCHOR-PUBLISH-FAILED or ALR-ANCHOR-SECONDARY-FAILED.
AS (ancillary service)An ERCOT reserve product sold as capacity. Contracts of service type ERCOT_AS are held at 0 kW (a capacity hold) until deployed. See Capacity holds.
AssetA market-model record in og.asset with class HOME_BANK or SUBSTATION, used for territory and economics. In dispatch terms every asset is a bank of one or more hubs.
AT_RISKA flag on an obligation (og.obligation.at_risk), not a state: delivery may fall short (energy, PQ or a sustained shortfall). Traced as AT_RISK / AT_RISK_CLEARED.
BankA group of hubs behind one distribution transformer bank, the unit of allocation and of BANK safe stops. The simulated fleet has 50 homes per bank at 600 kVA. A substation battery and a mobile unit are each a one-hub bank.
CalibrationA remote inverter recalibration command, gated by guardian G-25 (bounds, rate budget, never on a hub with an active non-default PQ envelope). Requested from the PQ page (Request calibration (step 1 of 2)) or by the PQ ladder. The automatic asset-drift sweep that would request it is off ([assets].drift_enabled = false).
Charge windowAn owner-set period (America/Chicago, may wrap midnight) when toll/regulated banks may charge from the grid; default 22:00-06:00 (D-30). Stored in og.owner_charge_window, edited on the Fleet page.
Bootstrapdeploy/scripts/bootstrap_from_scratch.sh: one idempotent script (phases a-l, --dry-run, --phase) that builds the whole solution on a fresh host or database, automating deploy/BOOTSTRAP.md steps 3-10. See Rebuild.
CheckpointA saved set of trace stream heads (og.trace_checkpoint). Pruning never deletes a trace row not covered by a checkpoint, so verification still passes after retention (K11).
CommitmentThe frozen kW that an obligation is committed to for one interval (og.commitment.committed_kw). Protected by the commitment lock (K13); changed only at a re-nomination point.
Commitment lockInvariant K13: committed deliveries run to completion; no mid-contract switching on price. See Commitments and the lock.
CONSERVATIVE postureA bank or zone where more than 5 % of a guardian tick's commands were vetoed. The engine reduces new dispatch there (og.scope_posture) and ALR-SCOPE-CONSERVATIVE is raised. Part of K7 escalation.
D-numbersOwner and lead decisions in docs/orchestrator/07-delivery/11-decision-log.md (D-4 to D-37 at r3.4.2), which override older spec text. For example D-19 (M1), D-28 (solar share), D-29 (utility toll), D-30 (charge windows), D-31 (trucks), D-32 (the zone blocks LZ_LCRA and LZ_RAYBN enabled), D-37 (those zones are regulated (NOIE) territory, unavailable until a contract is signed).
Dead letter (stop outbox)A stop outbox entry that failed permanently outbox_max_attempts (5) times. og-safestop skips it, traces it and raises the critical ALR-STOP-PUBLISH-DEAD-LETTER once, so later stops still go out. Broker outages never count toward the limit.
Device infoThe rating and location report a hub publishes on <root>/hub/<hub_id>/info. At r3.4 it is stored only in the og.hub.device_* columns (migration 0043) and never changes the seeded ratings, K1 reserve or location; a report whose hub differs from the topic, or for an unknown hub, is rejected. A mismatch with the seed raises ALR-DEVICE-RATING-MISMATCH. Every report is traced (DEVICE_INFO / DEVICE_INFO_REJECTED).
DAMERCOT's Day-Ahead Market. OpenGrid reads DAM ancillary-service clearing prices (np4-188-cd) to value AS offers; it does not submit DAM offers at r3.4 (ercot_mms is off).
Degraded modeA reduced way of operating when an input or process is lost (K7): NO_NEW_COMMITMENTS (a firm-blocking feed is stale), HOLD_LOCAL_AUTONOMY (engine down), HOLD (guardian down or verdict timeout), DIST_DEFERRAL_OPEN_LOOP (a bank's SCADA silent). See Degraded modes.
DIST_DEFERRALA distribution-deferral service that keeps a bank's loading under a limit. Its PI controller is the single integrating loop for bank kVA (K9).
DNP3A utility SCADA protocol. A scada_dnp3 adapter exists in opengrid.integrations and is tested against the simulator; the live SCADA source at r3.4 is MQTT (the default).
ECRSERCOT Contingency Reserve Service, an AS product. Its product rule sustains 60 min, which sets both the energy hold and the longest deployment.
EIAUS Energy Information Administration Open Data API, polled by og-feeds only as a standby source for ERCOT system load (np6-345-cd).
Engine (og-engine)The process that runs the 2 s loop: fleet flush, the 15-minute selector gate, the allocator, and the hand-off of command batches to the guardian. It also ingests telemetry and SCADA over MQTT.
EpochA strictly increasing counter carried on every command with its sequence number and lease; wrong-epoch commands are rejected (K6, guardian G-13).
F2 (rule fallback)The selector's deterministic greedy rule (firm, then AS, then market), used when the MILP is infeasible, times out without an acceptable gap or fails validation (plan_mode RULE_FALLBACK).
FeederA distribution circuit that serves several banks (5 per feeder in the simulated fleet). Guardian G-06, G-28 and G-32 check feeder ramp, thermal and reverse-flow limits.
Firmware campaignA staged hub firmware rollout (og.firmware_campaign). The guardian signs each update under G-36. A campaign over 50 hubs needs a second operator; at most 10 % of a bank or feeder updates at once; it halts on failure thresholds (2 hubs or 5 % by default). See Firmware.
FREE / REGULATEDThe two markets (D-20). FREE is the ERCOT competitive market; REGULATED is a vertically integrated utility as customer, with a utility_id. Stored on og.contract.market.
G-numbersGuardian check ids: G-01 reserve (and G-01-ENERGY), G-02 hub power, G-03 bank kVA, G-04 hub ramp, G-05 fleet ramp and stagger, G-06 feeder ramp ceiling, G-09 ledger version, G-13 sequence/epoch/lease, G-14 trace pre-image, G-15 L2 instruction, G-19 commitment lock, G-20 time quality, G-21 to G-25 power quality and calibration, G-26 to G-32 flow limits, G-33 territory, G-34 hub on bank, G-35 mobile charging, G-36 firmware. G-07, G-08, G-10 to G-12 and G-16 to G-18 are deferred. See G-codes.
GrantThe kW the allocator gives an obligation (or free headroom) on one bank in one cycle (og.grant). Grants become command items that the guardian checks.
Guardian (og-guardian)The independent safety process and only signer of power commands (K3). It re-checks every batch on its own database and telemetry reads, signs with Ed25519 what passes, vetoes what does not, and signs two-person safe-stop releases.
HeadroomCapacity not needed by any committed obligation in a cycle, offered to the ERCOT energy market (R-GRANT-HEADROOM) with a 300 s dwell and $5/MWh hysteresis.
Hold(1) A capacity hold: an AS award or toll granted 0 kW with its reservation and energy kept until called. (2) The degraded mode HOLD: hubs keep their last signed setpoint until the lease expires.
Hub stale / offlineA hub whose last telemetry is older than [health].hub_stale_s (25 s) is stale, older than [health].hub_offline_s (60 s) offline. At r3.4 the fleet twin, the health rules and the console badges all use these two values (hubs report every 10 s).
HubOne home battery site (hub-NNNNN) with one or two Base Power units (39.2 kWh / 11 kW each; dual-unit homes 78.4 kWh / 20 kW). The unit that receives signed commands and reports telemetry.
ICCPInter-Control Center Communications Protocol (TASE.2), used between control centres. A scada_iccp adapter exists, simulator-tested, not the live source at r3.4.
IEEE 2030.5A standard DER communication protocol. An ieee2030_5 adapter exists in opengrid.integrations; it is not the live source at r3.4.
InvoiceBilling output of og-settle in og.invoice_line. The table is insert-only at the database layer: a correction is a new row that supersedes the old one. Customers can dispute a line through the customer API (when enabled).
K-numbersThe canonical invariants K1 to K15 in docs/orchestrator/07-delivery/00-invariants.md. See Guarantees.
K7 escalationThe guardian's rule that repeated vetoes degrade instead of tripping: a bad tick (over 5 % vetoed) makes a scope CONSERVATIVE; three escalations request a scoped safe stop from a person (ALR-SAFE-STOP-REQUESTED); three good ticks clear it. Timeouts never count as vetoes.
L0 / L1 / L2, T1-T4Obligation tiers. L0 is safety, L1 the homeowner reserve, L2 grid authority (utility/ISO instructions); T1 to T4 are commercial tiers allocated lexicographically. Only L0/L1/L2 and physical infeasibility may reduce a commitment.
L2 instructionA utility or ISO instruction (LIMIT, BLOCK, ESTOP) received on <root>/scada/instruction/+. LIMIT is enforced by the allocator and guardian; BLOCK/ESTOP make og-safestop engage a BANK stop.
Late recordA manual target that reached og.trace more than 30 s after it was issued (for example a trace-journal replay of a confirm that answered 503). The engine never activates it and appends a cancel row (cancel_kind LATE_RECORD); its status is CANCELLED_LATE_RECORD.
LeaseHow long a hub may keep applying a signed setpoint (30 s by default). If no newer command arrives the hub stops applying it, which is how a lost engine degrades safely.
LedgerThe reservation store (og.reservation) with one writer, the engine, and a version checked by the guardian (G-09). It enforces one buyer per kW/kWh (K2).
Lifecycle (data lifecycle)The partition, rollup, cold-export and retention job, python -m opengrid.lifecycle run --cycle auto. At r3.4 it ships as og-lifecycle.service (oneshot, idle I/O) and og-lifecycle.timer (every 10 min), installed by deploy.sh but never enabled by it. See Data lifecycle.
LMPLocational marginal price. OpenGrid does not use nodal LMPs at r3.4; each bank is priced at its load zone's settlement point price (SPP).
M1The TDSP delivery charge: a flat $/kWh on energy drawn from the grid to charge, ERCOT competitive area only, per TDSP, from config/tdsp_tariffs.toml (for example Oncor 0.060295 $/kWh from 2026-09-01). See Market model.
Manual targetAn operator's confirmed setpoint for hubs, recorded as a MANUAL_TARGET trace event and ramped by the engine while its status is ACTIVE, until it expires (default 15 min, max 240), is cancelled by an operator or a covering safe stop, or is refused as a late record. See Manual targets.
MCPCMarket Clearing Price for Capacity: ERCOT's DAM price for an AS product, read from np4-188-cd. AS offers are valued at the latest MCPC only if it is at most 93600 s old.
MosquittoThe MQTT broker the processes and the simulators connect to (loopback port 1883, topic root og/v1). Development workspaces get their own users limited to ogtest/<ws>/# (D-13).
MQTTThe publish/subscribe protocol carrying hub telemetry (tel/#), SCADA (scada/#), utility instructions, signed commands and retained stop messages (stop/...). See MQTT topics.
Need basisA commitment where the committed kW is a reserved maximum and delivery follows the customer's measured need (profiles with MEASURED_FEEDBACK, such as DATA_CENTER and PIPELINE_AC; D-18). Built but switched off at r3.4.
NO_NEW_COMMITMENTSThe degraded mode entered when a feed that may block firm commitments goes stale. Existing commitments keep being served; the selector makes no new ones (R-DEGRADED-NO-NEW-COMMIT).
NOIENon-opt-in entity: a municipal or co-op utility outside retail choice that is not one of OpenGrid's regulated customers. A territory value that exists in code but is not used at r3.4.
NSPINERCOT Non-Spinning Reserve, an AS product. Its product rule sustains 240 min.
NWSUS National Weather Service API, polled hourly by og-feeds for weather inputs to the forecast.
ObligationA unit of work sold to a customer for a window (og.obligation), with a service type, tier and committed kW, moving through OFFERED, SELECTED, COMMITTED, DELIVERING, FULFILLED or SHORTFALL, SETTLED (or REJECTED/EXPIRED).
Operator actionA row in og.operator_action recording who did what, with a tier: PRE_AUTHORIZED, ENGAGE, TIER1 (one operator) or TIER2 (two operators, for example a safe-stop release).
OpportunityA candidate piece of work offered to the selector (og.opportunity): OFFERED, then SELECTED, REJECTED or EXPIRED.
Outbox (stop outbox)og.stop_outbox: og-safestop's durable queue of signed ENGAGE and RELEASE messages, written in the same transaction as the og.stop_event row and re-published (ENGAGEs first) until the broker acknowledges them, so a stop survives a broker outage. See also Dead letter.
P10 / P50 / P90Forecast quantiles (0.10, 0.50, 0.90). The selector optimises over all three scenarios.
PQ (power quality)Per-phase imbalance, voltage and frequency deviation and harmonic distortion at the point served. Each customer profile can carry a PQ envelope (invariant K14, guardian G-21 to G-24).
PQ ladderThe corrective sequence for a PQ drift on a committed obligation: rebalance phases, substitute hubs, recalibrate, adjust PF (recorded only), exclude a hub, then AT_RISK (R-PQ-DRIFT-AT-RISK). Inactive at r3.4 because site ingest and closed-loop control are off.
Proposal (two-step)The pattern used for every risky write: step 1 creates a proposal and changes nothing; step 2 confirms it. Proposals expire after 60 s (safe-stop proposals after 30 s).
RB-*A runbook id (for example RB-005 firmware cohort regression, RB-044 disk budget and retention, RB-071 ICCP link loss), defined in docs/orchestrator/02-architecture/06-platform-and-operations.md. The console's own procedures are under Playbooks.
Re-nomination pointAn agreed time (og.renomination_point) at which a commitment may be re-selected, confirmed or skipped. It is the only normal point at which a commitment can change.
REG-UP / REG-DOWNERCOT Regulation Up and Regulation Down (REGUP, REGDN). Recognised by the ERCOT market adapter and AS valuation; not a seeded demo product at r3.4.
ReservationA ledger row that ties power (POWER_KW) or energy (ENERGY_KWH) on a bank and interval to exactly one obligation (K2).
ReserveThe share of each home battery kept for the homeowner's own backup, 20 % of usable energy (D-25). Never discharged below by any command (K1).
RRSERCOT Responsive Reserve Service, an AS product; its clearing price series is shown on the Markets page.
RTMERCOT's Real-Time Market. OpenGrid prices headroom at the real-time settlement point price (np6-905-cd).
Safe stopA scoped (FLEET, ZONE, BANK) command that ramps hubs to 0 kW, engaged by og-safestop with its stop-only key and released only through the guardian with two operators (K8). See Safe stop.
SCADASupervisory control and data acquisition: the utility's measurements of banks and feeders (for example apparent power in kVA) and its instructions. At r3.4 SCADA arrives over MQTT from the simulator; silence raises ALR-SCADA-SILENT or ALR-SCADA-SILENT-BANK.
SCEDERCOT's Security-Constrained Economic Dispatch, which sets real-time prices about every 5 minutes. OpenGrid does not interact with SCED directly; it consumes the resulting prices.
SelectorThe planning optimiser (opengrid.selector) run at every 15-minute gate and on admission or re-nomination. It builds a HiGHS MILP over banks, 96 intervals and three scenarios, commits obligations and writes an og.plan.
Service profilePer-customer service settings (og.service_profile, config/service_profiles/*.toml), for example pipeline, data centre, large load, mobile storage, including the PQ envelope and setpoint source.
Settle (og-settle)The process that meters delivered intervals, settles them into P&L and invoice lines every 60 s, and hosts health evaluation, the invariant checker, trace pruning and anchoring.
SHORTFALLThe obligation state reached when a lock exception reduces delivery for 60 s (30 cycles) mid-window, or at window end. Delivery continues at the maximum feasible kW; settlement uses actual delivery.
SoCState of charge: the energy stored in a hub, reported in kWh (soc_kwh). Missing or stale SoC is treated as zero available for discharge.
Solar shareThe fraction of charging energy that came from solar (D-28), measured from hub telemetry, else ERCOT published solar, else a 30 % assumption. It reduces the grid-charged kWh that M1 applies to.
SPPSettlement point price. The live price of each bank is its own load zone's real-time SPP from np6-905-cd.
SubstitutionMoving part of an obligation's delivery from one hub to another hub of the same obligation (R-SUBSTITUTION). Allowed under K13 because the committed amount does not change.
TDSPTransmission and distribution service provider (Oncor, CenterPoint, AEP Central, AEP North, TNMP) in the ERCOT competitive area; the party whose delivery tariff sets M1.
TerritoryWhich utility (or the ERCOT competitive area) an asset sits in, resolved from its load zone via [zone_territory]. Governs invariant K15.
THDTotal harmonic distortion of current or voltage, one of the PQ measures in a customer's envelope (guardian G-22).
Toll (utility toll)A REGULATED_CAPACITY contract with variant TOLLING (D-29): one daily availability hold for the utility's window, discharged only when the utility calls, paid per kW-year.
Traceog.trace: the append-only record of every decision and operator action, chained per stream with SHA-256 (K10, K11). Verified every 300 s by the invariant checker and on demand with Run chain verify.
Trace journalA local append-only file ([trace].journal_path, shared by every process) that holds trace records the database could not take. The next trace write from any process replays it into og.trace automatically (K11 fail-safe).
Truck (mobile unit)A battery on a trailer (MOBILE_STORAGE) that charges only at its registered home station, never from the fleet (D-31, guardian G-35). Home stations come from config/service_profiles/mobile_storage_home_stations.toml (GET /og/api/fleet/home-stations); without a home-station schedule a truck never charges (fail closed).
VerdictThe guardian's answer for a command batch (og.verdict): PASS, PARTLY_VETOED, VETOED or TIMEOUT.
VetoA guardian refusal to sign an item or batch because a check failed, with a reason code. The allocator re-solves without the vetoed hubs. A veto is not a stop and not a timeout (K7).
Wear costThe battery degradation cost charged on discharged kWh in the objective, [allocator].wear_usd_per_kwh = 0.03.
ZoneAn ERCOT load zone (LZ_NORTH, LZ_SOUTH, LZ_HOUSTON, LZ_WEST, and LZ_AEN, LZ_CPS, LZ_LCRA, LZ_RAYBN): the unit of pricing, forecasting, territory and ZONE safe stops. The shipped simulator enables LZ_LCRA and LZ_RAYBN (500 hubs, 10 banks each); under D-37 they are regulated (NOIE) territory whose banks are unavailable (“Regulated market – no contract”). Production enables LZ_AEN through its own override.
Regulated market – no contractThe availability badge (og.bank.availability = UNAVAILABLE, reason REGULATED_NO_CONTRACT, D-37) on LZ_LCRA/LZ_RAYBN hubs and banks: Unavailable: regulated (NOIE) territory, so energy can't be sold into ERCOT, and there is no utility capacity contract to reserve it. Available once a contract is signed. The selector and allocator offer and plan nothing there (no charging either); telemetry, alerts, health, safe stop and firmware keep working. The APIs carry it as availability, availability_reason, availability_text.
Sample contractA contract named Sample Contract: … with is_sample set (D-37): a template modelled on the Austin Energy toll, shown as SAMPLE – INACTIVE. It is never active, never called, never billed.
