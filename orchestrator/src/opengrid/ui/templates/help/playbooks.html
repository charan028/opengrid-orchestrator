Playbooks: what to do when...

One playbook per situation. Each uses the same layout: how the situation shows on the console, who acts, what the system already does on its own, then the steps with the exact screen and button, what has to be confirmed, how to tell it is over, and when to escalate. Button labels are the ones in the r3.4 templates. Where a step cannot be done from the console at r3.4, the playbook says so and gives the API path or the deploy/RUNBOOK.md step instead.

The design runbooks RB-* in docs/orchestrator/02-architecture/05-failure-modes-and-recovery.md describe the target platform (for example NATS, EMQX, a separate Safe-Stop Authority host). The r3.4 deployment is a single base server with Postgres 17, Mosquitto and ten systemd units. The playbooks below cite an RB-* only for the intent and follow the r3.4 code and deploy/RUNBOOK.md for the actual commands.

Never print or paste the contents of /etc/opengrid/*.env, /etc/opengrid/htpasswd, the signing keys under /etc/opengrid or /root/opengrid-ui-credentials.txt. Scripts read them; people do not.

Who is who: personas and access

Every console user signs in through Apache Basic Auth. Apache forwards the account name to og-api together with a shared proxy secret, and og-api trusts the name only with that secret. An account without a role mapping is treated as a viewer. The footer of the left navigation shows "Role: operator" or "Role: viewer". The Help entry (this page) is at the bottom of the left navigation menu, just above that role footer, on every console page; each screen's Help link opens its own section here (/og/help#page-<screen>).

Normal operations and the daily plan

How it shows
Control room (/og/): header badge live; the story line ends "0 promises broken today"; the three "Promises kept" tiles (Reserve breaches, kWh sold twice, Commitment switches) read 0. The notification bell says "Nothing needs attention." except for the expected ALR-XFMR-UNMAPPED warnings (one per commanded bank until transformers are mapped).
Who acts
Operator on shift; viewers watch.
What the system does automatically
og-engine runs the 2 s allocator and the quarter-hour selector gates (LP optimizer over 96 quarter-hours, rule-based fallback); og-guardian checks and signs every batch; og-engine offers each active tolling contract's daily window ([contracts.tolling], 16:30-18:00 CT, today if not started plus tomorrow); og-settle meters, settles and runs the health evaluator every 5 s; the invariant and trace-chain checks run; the nightly backup runs at 03:00. There is no separate daily-plan approval step at r3.4: the "daily plan" is the selector's latest plan.

Steps (start of shift)

  1. /og/: read the story line, the "Promises kept" tiles and the KPIs. Open the bell and read the Safety, Degraded modes and Alerts groups.
  2. /og/health: every row in "Processes" has a recent heartbeat time; no ALR-PROCESS-DOWN; "Hub health" mostly Online.
  3. /og/markets: "Freshness & source status" shows each feed LIVE, failures 0, breaker closed.
  4. /og/dispatch: the "Opportunity pipeline" cards (no amber border), "Latest selector plan" (Mode, Gate, Horizon, Solver status), the "Ledger timeline" per scope (header View), and "AS awards & deployment" (awards held, Risk OK).
  5. Acknowledge what you have read: tick rows in the Alerts panel and press Acknowledge selected (N), then Acknowledge N in the dialog; or Ack on one row. Acknowledging records who looked; it never clears an alert.
  6. /og/billing: set From and To, then Run chain verify (or POST /og/api/trace/verify with body {}).
  7. /og/profitability: today's Net margin and "Forgone upside (lock)".
Confirmations required
None for reading. A bulk acknowledgement asks once.
Verify recovery
Not applicable; this is the baseline the other playbooks return to.
Escalate when
Any "Promises kept" tile is non-zero; ALR-RESERVE-BREACH or ALR-TRACE-VERIFY-FAILED is open; the selector plan shows "Rule-based fallback" repeatedly or ALR-SELECTOR-GATE-FAILED keeps adding rows.

Price spike or feed outage (stale feed, NO_NEW_COMMITMENTS)

How it shows
A spike: every load-zone line on /og/markets "Wholesale price ($/MWh)" and the Control room "Market ticker" jump; no alert is raised for the price itself. An outage: ALR-FEED-STALE (warning, "Feed ERCOT:np6-905-cd stale for over 2700s") or ALR-FEED-LGV-EXHAUSTED (critical, breaker open); on the price feed, the degraded mode Feed stale (NO_NEW_COMMITMENTS) shows in the bell as "Degraded mode: Feed stale" and as the page banner "Degraded: ...". Markets shows the feed's breaker OPEN and a rising failure count.
Who acts
Operator; admin if og-feeds itself is down or needs a restart.
What the system does automatically
Spike: committed obligations keep their kW (K13); only L0/L1/L2 overrides or proven infeasibility may reduce one, and the guardian re-checks any such claim (G-19). Outage: og-feeds opens the product's circuit breaker; EIA backs the ERCOT load feed only while that feed is unavailable. From r3.4 og-feeds polls only the products in [feeds.ercot].products, and a failed or breaker-skipped poll is retried after 5 minutes, doubling up to 60 minutes (never later than the product's own cadence), so a missed daily AS-price poll recovers within the hour instead of the next day. Only the feeds in [health] firm_blocking_feeds (default the price feed ERCOT:np6-905-cd) set NO_NEW_COMMITMENTS; a blocking feed with no status row at all counts as stale. While the mode is active og-engine skips intake (trace INTAKE_SKIPPED) and every selector gate selects nothing new; offers stay OFFERED; committed deliveries continue. Contract admission through the API is not blocked.

Steps

  1. Spike: open /og/dispatch and confirm every Committed/Delivering card keeps its kW and reads "Locked: this promise is kept even if a better price appears (K13)". Nothing to do; within a 30 s refresh /og/profitability shows the non-zero "Forgone upside (lock)".
  2. Outage: /og/markets "Freshness & source status": which feed, its mode (LIVE, SIM, HIST), age, failures, breaker.
  3. /og/health: "Feed freshness" and "Alerts" (filter Rule). Check "Processes" for the feeds heartbeat; a missing one also raises ALR-PROCESS-DOWN.
  4. Admin, if og-feeds is down or wedged: journalctl -u og-feeds --since -10min, then systemctl restart og-feeds. Do not point og-feeds at the market simulator without the lead's OK (deploy/README.md, "Switching live APIs vs. the market simulator").
  5. If the upstream (ERCOT) is down, wait: nothing new is committed, and what is committed is delivered. Tell the desk that new offers will sit in OFFERED.
Confirmations required
None; no operator action changes feed handling from the console.
Verify recovery
The breaker reads closed, the alert clears by itself, the "Feed stale" entry leaves the bell, and the next gate selects again ("Latest selector plan" gate time moves; OFFERED cards move to Selected).
Escalate when
The price feed stays stale into a day-ahead gate; the breaker re-opens after every close; authentication to ERCOT fails (treat as pb-security); a price move reduces any commitment (that would be a K13 breach).

AS award held, then an ERCOT deployment

How it shows
/og/dispatch, panel "AS awards & deployment": one row per awarded ERCOT_AS obligation with Obligation, Product (ECRS · 1 h hold, Non-Spin · 4 h hold), Committed, Energy held, State (held or deployed) and Risk (OK or AT_RISK). A held award short of energy raises ALR-ENERGY-SHORTFALL-RISK.
Who acts
Operator, on ERCOT's deployment instruction (received outside OpenGrid).
What the system does automatically
A held award is granted 0 kW (R-GRANT-AS-HOLD); its capacity stays out of sellable headroom and its homes keep enough energy above reserve for the whole product (ECRS 1 h, Non-Spin 4 h). The guardian signs the 0 kW hold only when its own reads show an ERCOT_AS award with no active deployment (G-19). While a deployment is active the allocator discharges the award up to its committed kW; at its end time the award returns to a 0 kW hold. ERCOT deployment instructions are not ingested automatically at r3.4: the market-simulator poller is off ([market_sim] as_deployment_poll = false).

Steps

  1. /og/dispatch, "AS awards & deployment": find the award's row; check State held and Risk OK.
  2. Press Deploy… on that row. It selects the award in the form below and focuses Duration.
  3. Set Duration (min) within the product (the hint reads "Up to 60 min for ECRS"; Non-Spin 240) and Reason, then Propose deployment.
  4. Read the dialog "Confirm ERCOT AS deployment" (step 1 of 2; nothing changes yet), then Confirm deployment. The result reads "Deployment <id> is active." and State turns deployed.
  5. Watch "Real-time grants & substitutions" and the ledger for the award's bank.
  6. To end early: Stop deploy on the row, dialog "Confirm stop deployment", Stop deployment. Result: "Deployment <id> stopped."
  7. API equivalent: POST /og/api/dispatch/as-deployments with {"obligation_id": "<id>", "duration_minutes": 15, "reason": "..."}; DELETE /og/api/dispatch/as-deployments/{deployment_id}; list with GET.
Confirmations required
One operator, one confirm dialog (no countdown). og-api refuses: a missing award (422), a fleet-wide deployment (409, it would need two people), an award not in COMMITTED/DELIVERING/SHORTFALL (409 "award is ..., not deployable"), a duration over the product (409), and a second deployment while one is active (409 "an active deployment already covers this obligation").
Verify recovery
After the end time or a stop, State reads held again and the grant returns to 0 kW. /og/billing "Trace explorer" shows AS_DEPLOYMENT and AS_DEPLOYMENT_END on stream operator_action:<user>.
Escalate when
The award is AT_RISK when ERCOT deploys it; the deployment is refused for a reason you cannot resolve; delivered kW stays well below committed kW during the deployment (see pb-at-risk).

Utility toll call (D-29)

How it shows
A tolling obligation (REGULATED_CAPACITY, contract variant TOLLING) appears as a card in the /og/dispatch pipeline; the "Ledger timeline" draws it in the "Held (AS/toll)" series and the text reads "N kW held for AS/tolling awards (reserved, 0 kW granted until deployed)". It does not appear in the "AS awards & deployment" table, which lists ERCOT_AS only.
Who acts
The utility calls (outside OpenGrid); an operator issues the call through the API.
What the system does automatically
og-engine offers one OFFERED opportunity per day for each ACTIVE tolling contract, for the [contracts.tolling] window (16:30-18:00 America/Chicago, days_ahead = 1, days = "ALL"), through normal admission. It is idempotent, never re-offers a window that overlaps a COMMITTED/DELIVERING obligation of the same contract (K13), and reserves nothing when the window length differs from the product rule's duration (90 min) or the rule has no size (logged by og-engine). The selector commits it as a regulated capacity hold; the allocator holds it at 0 kW until called; settlement pays availability. The Austin substation battery feeder has a 20 MW/min ramp ceiling (G-06, [guardian.feeder_ramp_ceiling_kw_per_min_by_feeder]).

Steps

  1. Find the tolling obligation id: the card's first line on /og/dispatch (the full id is in its tooltip), or GET /og/api/dispatch/opportunities.
  2. Not possible in the UI at r3.4. Issue the call from a shell as an operator: POST /og/api/dispatch/as-deployments with {"obligation_id": "<id>", "duration_minutes": 90, "reason": "<utility reference>"}. og-api stores the reason prefixed "utility call: " and returns kind: "UTILITY_CALL".
  3. Watch "Real-time grants & substitutions" and the ledger for the carrying banks.
  4. End early if the utility releases the call: DELETE /og/api/dispatch/as-deployments/{deployment_id}. Check what is active with GET /og/api/dispatch/as-deployments.
Confirmations required
None beyond the single API call (no two-step). The same refusals as an AS deployment apply; the cap is the obligation's product rule (TOLLING 90 min).
Verify recovery
The deployment disappears from the GET list at its end; the grant returns to 0 kW; the trace shows AS_DEPLOYMENT with kind UTILITY_CALL and the operator action UTILITY_CALL:<id>.
Escalate when
No tolling obligation exists for today's window (check og-engine's log for "tolling window does not match the product rule duration" or "no sized product rule"); og-api answers "award is OFFERED, not deployable" (the selector has not committed it); delivery falls short during the window.

Commitment AT_RISK or SHORTFALL: substitution and restore

How it shows
On /og/dispatch the card gets an amber border, with a falling "energy margin" and "depletes in"; ALR-ENERGY-SHORTFALL-RISK (critical) opens when energy is the cause. A SHORTFALL card moves to "Fulfilled / shortfall" and reads "Delivered short; penalty applies" although delivery continues.
Who acts
Operator.
What the system does automatically
Every 2 s the engine checks that committed hubs hold enough energy above reserve for the rest of the window; delivery moves to other hubs (grant kind substitution). A shortfall held 60 s moves the obligation to SHORTFALL with R-COMMIT-LOCK-INFEASIBLE or an L0/L1/L2 override reason. Decision D-17: it keeps receiving the maximum feasible kW for the rest of its window (never 0, never stopped), the full commitment returns at the earliest feasible interval, and it settles on what was delivered. The guardian re-checks every reduction claim (G-19). No home is discharged below reserve (K1, G-01).

Steps

  1. Note the obligation id from the card. In the header set Ledger to Feeder segment (bank) for the carrying bank and press View; read "Real-time grants & substitutions".
  2. Find the cause: /og/health Alerts and Hub health (comms, see pb-comms-loss); /og/fleet filtered by that bank (SoC, Health, Activity); a SCADA limit (see pb-bank-overload).
  3. Read the history in /og/billing "Trace explorer": stream shortfall-<obligation id> (class ALLOCATOR_SHORTFALL) and the obligation's own stream (AT_RISK, AT_RISK_CLEARED, the SHORTFALL transition).
  4. Fix the cause where you can (restore comms, end an operator target that is holding hubs, see pb-manual-target). Restoration of the commitment is automatic: there is no "restore" button or API at r3.4.
Confirmations required
None for the automatic path; any manual target you use needs its own two-step confirm.
Verify recovery
The amber border goes, ALR-ENERGY-SHORTFALL-RISK clears (og-engine clears it), grants return to the committed kW, and the trace shows AT_RISK_CLEARED. A SHORTFALL state stays until the window closes, by design.
Escalate when
A firm customer (DIST_DEFERRAL, PARTNER_CAPACITY, ERCOT_AS, tolling) is in SHORTFALL; the energy margin is negative close to the window; ALR-RESERVE-BREACH opens. DOC1 (R2) lists two gaps to watch for: the "Commitment-lock events (K13)" table may stay empty (use the trace), and a delivery cut by a utility L2 instruction may not return to full when the instruction lifts (watch the grants, not the card).

Hub or zone comms loss (hubs offline)

How it shows
/og/fleet: the summary counts (Online, Stale, Offline / fault) and the Health column; the hub drawer's "Last communication" and its age badge. A hub turns stale after [health] hub_stale_s (25 s) and offline after hub_offline_s (60 s) without telemetry. At r3.4 the Fleet summary, the age badges, the fleet twin and the Health rules all use these same two values, so a hub reporting every 10 s no longer flickers stale between reports. /og/health "Hub health" strip; ALR-HUB-OFFLINE-RATIO per zone (warning over 5% offline plus fault, critical over 20%); ALR-SIM-OFFLINE (critical) when no hub telemetry and no SCADA reading has arrived for sim_offline_s (90 s).
Who acts
Operator; admin for the broker or simulators.
What the system does automatically
Hubs hold their last setpoint for the lease plus hold (about 35 s), then serve their own homes (local autonomy). Offline hubs drop out of sellable capacity; delivery is substituted to other hubs, and a loss that cannot be replaced becomes a best-effort SHORTFALL (pb-at-risk). MQTT clients reconnect by themselves (counter og_mqtt_reconnects_total). Stop messages are retained, so a hub that reconnects still receives an engaged stop.

Steps

  1. /og/fleet: tick Zone and Health (OFFLINE) and press Apply filters; open a hub row for its drawer.
  2. Decide the scale: one hub, one bank, one zone, or the whole fleet. Fleet-wide silence with ALR-SIM-OFFLINE points at the broker, og-engine (it writes telemetry and SCADA readings, so its outage also raises this alert) or the simulators.
  3. Admin: systemctl is-active mosquitto og-engine og-sim-fleet og-sim-scada; journalctl -u og-engine --since -10min. On the simulated fleet, check /ogsim/ for an active comms_loss anomaly (for example scenario demo-03-zone-comms-loss) and stop it.
  4. Follow any affected obligation on /og/dispatch as in pb-at-risk.
Confirmations required
None. A safe stop is not needed for comms loss alone.
Verify recovery
Hubs read online again, the ratio alert clears when the zone is at or below 5%, grants return to the carrying banks.
Escalate when
A zone passes 20% (critical); the whole fleet is silent; the broker does not come back (pb-broker-db); a real field outage is suspected (call Base field services).

Bank overload or SCADA silent

How it shows
ALR-SCADA-OVERLOAD per bank: warning when the latest SCADA apparent power exceeds the bank's kVA rating (600 kVA), critical above 120%. ALR-SCADA-SILENT (critical) when no SCADA bank reading has arrived for scada_silent_s, and ALR-SCADA-SILENT-BANK (warning) per silent bank while others report; the degraded mode SCADA silent (DIST_DEFERRAL_OPEN_LOOP) shows in the bell. Neither is raised before the first reading ever arrives.
Who acts
Operator; the utility (for its own feeder); admin for the SCADA link or simulator.
What the system does automatically
Overload: the DIST_DEFERRAL grant on the bank rises (more discharge relieves the segment) while committed kW stay unchanged; the guardian's flow checks (G-26 to G-33) bound every setpoint; a utility LIMIT instruction is applied by og-engine. SCADA silent: shown and recorded only. Nothing acts on it at r3.4; the DIST_DEFERRAL loop keeps using the last SCADA reading.

Steps

  1. /og/health, Alerts: filter Rule ALR-SCADA-OVERLOAD (or ALR-SCADA-SILENT); note the bank and the percentage in the summary.
  2. /og/dispatch: Ledger Feeder segment (bank), that bank, View; check the DIST_DEFERRAL grant and the committed cards.
  3. If the overload persists at critical, call the utility. A safe stop on the bank removes OpenGrid's dispatch support there; it does not reduce the bank's own load, so use it only for a device or safety concern (pb-safe-stop).
  4. SCADA silent, admin: check og-sim-scada (simulated SCADA) or the real SCADA link (RB-023 intent). Treat DIST_DEFERRAL decisions as open loop until readings return.
Confirmations required
None, unless you engage a safe stop.
Verify recovery
The overload alert clears when the reading is at or under the rating; SCADA silent clears when a reading arrives (og-settle clears it), and the bell's "Degraded mode: SCADA silent" disappears.
Escalate when
Critical overload for more than a few minutes; ALR-BANK-UNMAPPED-TOPOLOGY is open (the bank has no feeder, so the guardian cannot run G-06/G-28/G-32 for it; with [guardian.flow] fail_closed_missing_topology = true it vetoes any batch that raises that bank's net setpoint, BANK_TOPOLOGY_UNMAPPED): fix the bank's feeder_id through a topology re-seed; SCADA silent during a DIST_DEFERRAL delivery window; the utility issues a LIMIT with no expiry.

Guardian veto burst: K7 conservative, then a safe-stop request

How it shows
ALR-SCOPE-CONSERVATIVE (warning): the bell's Safety group reads "N scope(s) held conservative" and links to the Health alerts filtered on that rule. Then ALR-SAFE-STOP-REQUESTED (critical): the Safety group lists "Guardian requests a safe stop: bank bank-007" with a Review (two-step) button, and the critical strip under the header shows it with a View link. The hub drawer's "Verdict" shows the last command's outcome (for example VETOED).
Who acts
Operator decides; a second operator only if a stop is later released.
What the system does automatically
Per bank and per zone, every 2 s tick: more than 5% of a scope's commands vetoed puts it CONSERVATIVE (the engine stops selling spot headroom there; committed deliveries continue). An escalation count of 3 raises the stop request and records an unconfirmed safe-stop proposal. The guardian never engages a stop itself (K8). The scope returns to normal after 3 consecutive good ticks (at most 2.5% vetoed) or 30 ticks with no commands; the count then drops by one per good tick. The posture lives in og-guardian's memory, so after a guardian restart a scope may stay CONSERVATIVE until it recovers again.

Steps

  1. Open the bell; note the scope. On /og/fleet, filter by that bank or zone and open a few hub drawers (Verdict, Fault, Last communication).
  2. Find the rule ids: /og/billing "Trace explorer", class GUARDIAN_VERDICT. Typical causes are bad telemetry, a flow limit (G-26 to G-33), or an operator target pushing hubs; cancel such a target with Cancel target (pb-manual-target).
  3. If the cause is a device or area fault that needs dispatch stopped: press Review (two-step). It opens /og/fleet with the "Safe stop" card prefilled (scope, scope id, reason "Guardian escalation: safe stop requested") and the note "Prefilled from the guardian's safe-stop request...". Continue with pb-safe-stop.
  4. If not, do nothing: with the cause gone the escalation clears on its own.
Confirmations required
Only if you stop: propose, then confirm within 30 s.
Verify recovery
Both alerts clear (og-guardian clears them) and the Safety group empties. The guardian's unconfirmed proposal stays in the operator-action log and is never acted on.
Escalate when
Stop requests recur on the same scope; ALR-CLOCK-QUALITY is open (every verdict is TIMEOUT while the clock is off, G-20); vetoes spread across zones. Note: ALR-GUARDIAN-TIMEOUT-RATE is never raised with the shipped config ([health] guardian_metrics_url is not set).

DEMO-2 step 15 triggers the burst with a loop of POST /og/api/fleet/command confirms expecting 409 vetoes. At r3.4 a confirmed manual command returns 202 RAMPING and the guardian judges each engine step instead, so that loop no longer shows a row of 409s.

Emergency safe stop and the two-person release

How it shows
Engaged: the result badge ENGAGED "Safe stop engaged for bank/bank-022. Trace ..."; the bank's hubs drop toward 0 kW on /og/fleet. Pending releases appear in the "Release request id" list.
Who acts
Any operator engages. Release: operator 1 requests, a different authorised operator (today og-op-a / og-op-b) approves.
What the system does automatically
og-safestop signs the stop with its own stop-only key and publishes a retained message per scope (og/v1/stop/<scope>/<id>/<stop_id>, event og.stop_event). While a bank is stopped the guardian signs no command for it, so its hubs get no new setpoint. A stop never forces a home below reserve. og-safestop works with og-engine and og-guardian down; it needs og-api, Postgres and Mosquitto. Every accepted ENGAGE or RELEASE is written together with its stop outbox entry (og.stop_outbox) in one transaction and republished until the broker acknowledges it, including after a broker outage; ENGAGEs go before older RELEASEs. A redelivered utility instruction whose stop was recorded but never queued is re-queued under the same stop id. An entry that fails permanently 5 times is dead-lettered: skipped, traced, and ALR-STOP-PUBLISH-DEAD-LETTER (critical) is raised once, so later stops still go out. Engaging a stop also cancels every manual target it covers (status CANCELLED_BY_SAFE_STOP); the target does not resume after the release. The guardian signs a release only after its own checks; og-safestop's key can never sign one.

Steps: engage

  1. /og/fleet, "Actions", card "Safe stop": Scope (Fleet, Zone, Bank), Scope id (empty for Fleet), Reason.
  2. Press Propose safe stop (step 1 of 2). The dialog "Confirm scoped safe stop" shows the API's summary and "Expires in 30s".
  3. Press Engage safe stop within the countdown (focus starts on Cancel; Tab moves to the confirm button). Results: ENGAGED, TIMEOUT (no confirmation from og-safestop), EXPIRED (propose again), FAILED.
  4. API: POST /og/api/safestop with {"scope": "bank", "scope_id": "bank-034", "reason": "..."}, then POST /og/api/safestop/{proposal_id}/confirm within 30 s (later answers 409 "proposal expired, propose again").

Steps: release (two people)

  1. Operator 1, card "Release a safe stop": Scope, Scope id, Reason, Request release (operator 1). Result REQUESTED with the request id and "a second operator approves it below within 60 s".
  2. Operator 2 (different account): pick the request in "Release request id" (it lists id, scope, requester and age), press Review and approve (operator 2), read "Approve safe-stop release", press Approve release.
  3. Results: RELEASED "Safe stop released for ..."; PENDING (approved, the guardian has not signed within og-api's 10 s wait); REFUSED "The requesting operator cannot approve their own release." (the request stays valid for another operator); EXPIRED; FAILED.
  4. API: POST /og/api/safestop/{scope}/{scope_id}/release (operator 1), POST /og/api/safestop/release/{proposal_id}/approve (operator 2: 200 released, 202 pending, 403 same person, 410 expired).
Confirmations required
Engage: two steps by one operator, confirm within 30 s ([safestop].confirm_window_s). Release: two different authorised operators; the approval must be fresh (at most 300 s).
Verify recovery
After release the bank's hubs take commands again (drawer "Last command" changes; P follows the allocator). The trace shows SAFE_STOP_ENGAGE, SAFE_STOP_RELEASE (on the approver's stream, with the requester in the payload) and the guardian's signed release (GUARDIAN_VERDICT).
Escalate when
The guardian refuses the release (GUARDIAN_VERDICT kind=STOP_RELEASE outcome=REFUSED: unauthorised operator, same person twice, stale approval, no stop engaged, a stop engaged after the approval, a utility-initiated stop, or an active ESTOP/BLOCK in scope): the stop stays engaged; fix the cause and request again. If og-api is down no stop can be requested from the console or API; the admin restores og-api first (systemctl restart og-api). ALR-STOP-PUBLISH-DEAD-LETTER is open: that stop message was never published, so the hubs did not receive it. Read the entry's last_error in og.stop_outbox and journalctl -u og-safestop, fix the cause, then engage the stop again from Fleet (it gets a new stop id; recommended practice, the system does not do this automatically) and tell the lead.

Manual command or target: ramp, expiry, cancel

How it shows
After confirm: badge RAMPING "Ramping N hubs to X kW ... Holds until ... Trace ...", a progress bar and "Now: ..."; the Hubs table bar reads "N under an operator target" and each row "target X kW"; the hub drawer shows "Operator target X kW until ... (by ...: reason)".
Who acts
Operator.
What the system does automatically
Confirm records a MANUAL_TARGET trace event (reason code MANUAL_OPERATOR). The confirm succeeds only once that event is durably in og.trace; otherwise it answers 503 and nothing ramps. og-engine then ramps each hub toward the target at the hub's ramp limit every cycle (G-04), every step signed by the guardian; the guardian accepts the override only from its own read of live targets (G-19, R-OPERATOR-OVERRIDE). Engine, API and guardian share one status rule (core.manual_targets.effective_targets): ACTIVE, EXPIRED, CANCELLED_BY_OPERATOR, CANCELLED_BY_SAFE_STOP (a covering stop engaged at or after the target, or still engaged) or CANCELLED_LATE_RECORD (the target reached the trace more than 30 s after it was issued, for example a journal replay of a confirm that failed; it is never activated). Only ACTIVE targets show as markers and ramps, and the ramp display stops when the target ends. At expires_at (1 to 240 min, default 15) the hubs return to normal dispatch on the next cycle. A newer target on a hub replaces the older one. See Manual targets.

Steps: one hub or one bank

  1. /og/fleet, card "Command one hub": Hub id (its bank fills in) or Bank id, Setpoint (kW) (sign: + charges, − discharges), Expires in (min), Reason.
  2. Propose (step 1 of 2). Dialog "Confirm manual command", summary "Set <target> to X kW (reason)", "Expires in 60s".
  3. Send command. Results: RAMPING; NOT RECORDED (the API answered 503 because the trace store was unavailable; nothing is ramping, propose again to retry); EXPIRED (propose again); FAILED.
  4. To end early: Cancel target under the ramp status. Results: CANCELLED "Target cancelled for N hub(s); they return to normal dispatch."; 409, the target is no longer active, with the reason (expired, cancelled by operator, cancelled by safe stop or cancelled late record) and nothing to cancel; UNKNOWN (404, the API does not know this trace id); NOT RECORDED (503, the target is still running; press Retry cancel); FAILED.

Steps: a selection

  1. Select hubs: tick table rows, Select this page, Select all N matching, or on the "Fleet map" Select an area (drag; Shift adds). Clear selection empties it; the sticky bar offers Command selection….
  2. Card "Command selection": Setpoint (kW), Expires in, Reason, Propose for selection (step 1 of 2). Dialog "Confirm command for N hubs".
  3. If any selected hub serves a customer, is delivering, faulted, offline, under a critical alert or at reserve, tick "I understand this overrides what these hubs are doing now: ..." before Send to selection will act.
  4. If the API asks for it, badge SECOND CONFIRM: press Confirm again and send to the selection. Results: RAMPING, or PASS / PARTIAL with each refused hub and its rule ids.
  5. API: POST /og/api/fleet/command then /og/api/fleet/command/{proposal_id}/confirm; bulk POST /og/api/fleet/commands/bulk then .../bulk/{proposal_id}/confirm; list GET /og/api/fleet/manual-targets (each hub's newest target with its status, stop_event_id and cancelled_by; add include_expired=true for EXPIRED ones); cancel POST /og/api/fleet/manual-targets/{trace_id}/cancel (200, 404 unknown, 409 no longer active, 503 not recorded).
Confirmations required
Two steps within 60 s; a selection that overrides customer-serving or faulted hubs needs the acknowledgement tick and may need a second confirm.
Verify recovery
The P column converges on the target; after expiry or cancel the target marker disappears and GET /og/api/fleet/manual-targets shows the hubs with a status other than ACTIVE (expired targets only with include_expired=true).
Escalate when
A ramp never moves (check the drawer's Verdict for vetoes); a confirm keeps answering NOT RECORDED (the database or trace store is down, see pb-broker-db); the target starves a committed delivery (see pb-at-risk); a charging target on a truck is vetoed by G-35 (expected, pb-trucks).

Firmware rollout: staged, halted, retry, rollback

How it shows
/og/fleet: the "FW version" and "HW rev" columns and filters, and "FW ≠ (out of date)"; the drawer's "HW rev" and "FW". Alerts ALR-FIRMWARE-CAMPAIGN-STARTED and ALR-FIRMWARE-CAMPAIGN-COMPLETED (info), ALR-FIRMWARE-HUB-FAILED (warning, one per hub failed for good), ALR-FIRMWARE-CAMPAIGN-HALTED (critical).
Who acts
Operator proposes and confirms; a second operator approves when required.
What the system does automatically
og-engine's executor works through the waves (default canary of 1 hub, then 25% steps; next wave only when the current one has finished; a canary with no success halts). Caps: at most 10% of a bank's and of a feeder's hubs at once. Halt at 2 terminal failures or 5%. Every attempt is signed by the guardian after G-36 (campaign RUNNING, hub online and not stopped, not serving a committed obligation in the next 15 min, SoC at least reserve plus 10%, catalogued image and sha256 for its hardware, caps). A refusal is a hold, re-checked after 60 s. Retries: up to 3 attempts with backoff for no ack, silence or download errors; never for a sha256 mismatch, incompatibility, bad signature or install failure.

Steps

  1. Not possible in the UI at r3.4: the Fleet card "Firmware update" shows a disabled Firmware update — available after R3.1 button, although the API is served. Use the API as an operator.
  2. Check the catalogue: GET /og/api/firmware/catalogue. The r3.4 catalogue is the demo set for the simulated fleet (placeholder sha256 values); never use it for real batteries.
  3. Propose: POST /og/api/firmware/campaigns with name, target_version, selection (hub ids or banks, zones, feeders, hardware revisions, current versions), waves, caps, optional window, reason. Hubs already on the version, or with no catalogue entry, are SKIPPED.
  4. Confirm within 60 s: POST /og/api/firmware/campaigns/{id}/confirm.
  5. If requires_second_operator (more than 50 hubs, override_committed, or allow_downgrade): a different operator calls POST .../{id}/approve.
  6. Watch: GET .../campaigns/{id} (per-hub state and counts) and GET .../campaigns/{id}/events?after=<event_id>.
  7. Pause, resume or abort: POST .../{id}/pause, /resume, /abort (a hub mid-install cannot be recalled).
  8. Halted: read the failures first (RB-005: compare the failed cohort with the control cohort). Retry: POST .../{id}/retry-failed, then .../retry-failed/{proposal_id}/confirm; the campaign becomes PAUSED, resume it deliberately. Roll back a hub: POST .../{id}/hubs/{hub_id}/rollback, then .../rollback/{proposal_id}/confirm.
Confirmations required
Propose plus confirm within 60 s; second operator (never the proposer or confirmer) when flagged; retry and rollback are two-step.
Verify recovery
ALR-FIRMWARE-CAMPAIGN-COMPLETED; the FW version filter shows the target on the selected hubs; no open ALR-FIRMWARE-HUB-FAILED.
Escalate when
ALR-FIRMWARE-CAMPAIGN-HALTED; a canary fails; failures cluster on one hardware revision (suspect image: roll back affected hubs).

Broker or database outage: reconnect, journal, stop outbox

How it shows
Broker down: telemetry stops, so ALR-SIM-OFFLINE and ALR-HUB-OFFLINE-RATIO open and live badges read reconnecting. Database down: console pages and the API fail; no health alert is raised or cleared (the evaluator in og-settle needs Postgres) and the degraded modes freeze.
Who acts
Admin, with the lead; operators watch.
What the system does automatically
Hubs hold about 35 s, then serve their homes. MQTT clients reconnect on their own (og_mqtt_reconnects_total). og-safestop records every accepted ENGAGE or RELEASE together with its og.stop_outbox entry in one transaction and republishes them (ENGAGEs first) once the broker is back; a broker outage never counts toward the dead-letter limit. Trace writes fall back to the local append-only journal [trace].journal_path (/var/lib/opengrid/trace_journal.jsonl, fsynced per line) and drain into Postgres automatically when it returns (K11). While the database is down a manual-target confirm or cancel answers 503 (NOT RECORDED) and nothing ramps; a target that reaches the trace later by journal replay is refused by the engine as a late record (CANCELLED_LATE_RECORD).

Steps (admin, root; deploy/RUNBOOK.md)

  1. Status: systemctl is-active mosquitto postgresql@17-main; journalctl -u mosquitto or the Postgres log /var/log/postgresql/postgresql-17-main.log.
  2. Restart by exact unit name (never pkill): systemctl restart mosquitto or systemctl start postgresql@17-main; then the og units if they exited (systemctl start opengrid.target ogsim.target).
  3. Postgres data directory suspect (I/O errors, will not start): follow deploy/ha/FAILOVER.md (manual promotion of the hot standby on port 5433, repoint [postgres], verify the trace chain), or restore the latest dump from /srv/ogbackup into a scratch database first (RUNBOOK "Backup and restore").
  4. After recovery: POST /og/api/trace/verify; confirm the trace journal has drained; check that every stop engaged during the outage reached its hubs (/og/fleet, P near 0 kW on the stopped scope) and that no ALR-STOP-PUBLISH-DEAD-LETTER is open; re-propose any manual target whose confirm answered NOT RECORDED.
Confirmations required
The lead's OK before a failover or a restore (both are destructive if done out of order).
Verify recovery
All units active; curl -fsS http://localhost:8080/og/api/health succeeds; heartbeats current on /og/health; alerts clear; trace verify passed: true.
Escalate when
The trace chain does not verify after a promotion or restore (stop and involve the lead before og-engine writes new rows); the journal does not drain; the broker keeps dropping clients (RB-015 intent).

Disk or performance degradation

How it shows
ALR-CYCLE-P99 (engine 2 s cycle p99 over its 500 ms budget for 3 reads) and ALR-CYCLE-P99-APPROACHING (80-100% of it); ALR-SETTLE-STALLED (settlement not completed for over 180 s); ALR-SELECTOR-GATE-FAILED (including solver timeouts). The "Cycle latency (p50/p99)" panel on /og/health is not wired to data at r3.4; the numbers are on the loopback metrics http://localhost:9101/metrics (og_engine_cycle_latency_ms).

Admin; operator reports.
What the system does automatically
Each unit runs under its systemd memory limit (MemoryMax/MemoryHigh, deploy/README.md). Retention prunes by class ([retention]: telemetry 14 days, feed observations 30, command trace 30, operator actions 400). Logs rotate daily, 14 kept. Backups keep 7 days on their own volume (/srv/ogbackup).

Steps (admin)

  1. curl -s http://localhost:9101/metrics | grep og_engine_cycle_latency_ms; df -h (Postgres data is in /srv/pgdata/17/main); journalctl -u og-engine -u og-guardian -u og-settle -u og-api --since -10min -o cat | grep -E '"ERROR"|Traceback'.
  2. Disk pressure: check retention (GET /og/api/retention; an operator may change a class with PUT /og/api/retention, traced as CONFIG_CHANGE, effective on the next prune); old releases beyond the last 5 are pruned by deploy.sh.
  3. Restart a leaking unit only outside a FIRM/AS delivery window, and preferably right after a Postgres checkpoint complete line (RUNBOOK).
Confirmations required
None in the console; follow the RUNBOOK caution on restarts during delivery.
Verify recovery
p99 back under budget; the alerts clear; the selector plan's "Solve time" normal.
Escalate when
p99 stays over budget (late commands); settlement stalls repeatedly; a volume nears full (RB-044 and RB-045 intent).

The host or its disks are lost, or a clean re-install is required.

Admin / release manager with the lead.
What the system does automatically

Security event: a denied action, key or secret rotation

How it shows
A write answered 403 (for example "Operator role required to acknowledge alerts."); a guardian refusal (GUARDIAN_VERDICT outcome REFUSED, reason such as OPERATOR_NOT_AUTHORISED); the banner "... no identity reached the console" when og-api is reached directly on its port; ALR-TRACE-VERIFY-FAILED, ALR-ANCHOR-PUBLISH-FAILED, ALR-TRACE-VERDICT-WRITE-FAILED.
Who acts
Operator reports; admin and lead investigate and rotate.
What the system does automatically
og-api believes an identity only with Apache's proxy secret; unknown accounts are viewers; every operator action is traced on operator_action:<user>; hubs execute only guardian-signed commands for their current lease epoch (K3, K6); og-safestop's key can only stop; the trace is hash-chained and checked every 5 minutes.

Steps

  1. Denied action: confirm who and what in /og/billing "Trace explorer" (filter the class, e.g. GUARDIAN_VERDICT) and in journalctl -u og-api. A legitimate user with the wrong role: fix the account (pb-personas). An unknown user or repeated attempts: tell the lead.
  2. Password rotation: htpasswd -B /etc/opengrid/htpasswd <user>; update the credentials file by hand; htpasswd -D removes an account (also remove it from [api.roles], stop_release_authorised_operators and the Apache Require user line; deploy; apache2ctl configtest before systemctl reload apache2).
  3. Service secrets (secrets.env, api_keys.env, ai_agent.env): edit on the server, restart the units that read them. Never print them.
  4. Signing keys (guardian, safe-stop, trace anchor): no rotation procedure is implemented at r3.4. RB-057 describes the intent; a guardian key change also needs every verifier (hubs, og-safestop) to trust the new public key. Treat as a lead-owned change.
  5. Chain failure: run POST /og/api/trace/verify; note first_broken stream and sequence; do not edit rows.
Confirmations required
The lead for any account, secret or key change.
Verify recovery
The affected user can do exactly what the role allows; trace verify passed: true; no anchor alerts.
Escalate when
Any sign of a forged or unsigned command, a broken chain, a leaked secret or key, or a spoofed identity header: immediately.

Confirmations required
Owner go-live for the customer API; the lead for account and config changes.
Verify recovery
The contract lists in GET /og/api/contracts; its first opportunity moves through the pipeline; with the API on, GET /og/api/customer/me answers for the new account.
Escalate when
Admission rejects a contract you expect to pass; anyone asks to enable the customer API without the owner's confirmation.
