Architecture

OpenGrid runs as six orchestrator processes (og-* systemd units grouped by opengrid.target) that share one PostgreSQL database (schema og) and one Mosquitto MQTT broker. Apache sits in front and handles authentication. The integration simulators (og-sim-*, grouped by ogsim.target) play the part of the hub fleet, SCADA, the market data sources and the customer operators. Everything below reflects release r3.4 (tag r3.3 plus the r3.4 fixes).

Functional flow

  1. Market data. og-feeds polls the ERCOT public API, EIA v2 and NWS (or og-sim-market in development) on each source's own schedule and writes normalized points to feed_obs with a quality flag (GOOD, ESTIMATED, STALE). feed_status tracks failures and the breaker. A feed that goes STALE makes the health evaluator set the NO_NEW_COMMITMENTS degraded mode; intake is then skipped.
  2. Forecast. Inside the same og-feeds loop, opengrid.forecast recomputes quantile forecasts (p10, p50, p90, firm fitness) every 15 minutes into forecast.
  3. Selector gate. og-engine's gate scheduler starts gates on the 15-minute boundary (a day-ahead plan once a day, intraday otherwise), on admission of a new opportunity, and on a due renomination point. Gates run as one background task so they never hold the 2 s tick. The model is solved with HiGHS; if it is infeasible, times out or fails validation, the rule fallback F2 is used. Selected candidates go through selector.commit: obligations move OFFERED to SELECTED to COMMITTED and the ledger writes reservation and commitment together (K2 one buyer per capacity, K13 commitment lock). Results are kept in plan, plan_value, plan_energy_value and plan_shadow_obligation.
  4. Allocator. Every 2 s og-engine advances the obligation lifecycle and runs allocator.run_cycle: utility L2 instructions cap capability first, energy is capped over the lease duration, tiers are served in strict order, each obligation is water-filled across eligible hubs, and remaining headroom may follow price. Grants are written to grant. For each bank the engine writes the trace pre-image first, then inserts command_batch, then sends NOTIFY og_command_batch. If the guardian heartbeat is missing, the engine holds and proposes nothing.
  5. Guardian. og-guardian evaluates each new batch against its own reads (G-20 clock check first, then the other G-checks independently). PASS signs the batch with Ed25519 and renews hub leases; VETOED, PARTLY_VETOED and TIMEOUT are holds, never a step to zero for firm obligations (K7). The engine re-solves without vetoed hubs. Sustained vetoes move a bank or zone to CONSERVATIVE in scope_posture.
  6. Hubs. The signed batch goes out on cmd/<bank>/batch and leases on lease/<hub> (retained). Each hub verifies the signature and the epoch, seq and lease freshness, then applies the setpoint or keeps holding the last signed one when the lease expires.
  7. Telemetry. Hubs publish tel/... every 10 s ([fleet].telemetry_interval_s) and an ack/<hub> per command. og-engine updates the in-memory fleet twin and a separate periodic task flushes hub_state, telemetry and command_ack. The guardian keeps its own telemetry cache and does not trust the engine's copy. A hub is stale after [health].hub_stale_s (25 s) and offline after hub_offline_s (60 s); the fleet twin, the health rules and the console use the same two values. The next allocator cycle reads this state.
  8. Settle. Every 60 s og-settle meters pending obligation intervals from telemetry (meter_interval), scores performance, writes versioned invoice_line rows and pnl (including the delivery charge where configured), and closes FULFILLED or SHORTFALL obligations to SETTLED.
  9. Safe-stop path. An operator stop is two-step: POST /og/api/safestop then /confirm; og-api passes both to og-safestop through NOTIFY og_safestop_request, and a single message can never stop the fleet. Utility BLOCK or ESTOP instructions trigger a bank stop directly. og-safestop writes stop_event and stop_outbox in one transaction, then publishes the retained stop/<scope>/<id> message. Release needs two operators and a guardian signature. See Safe stop.
  10. Trace journal. Every decision is appended to trace as a per-stream hash chain and mirrored to the local journal file. og-settle writes checkpoints, prunes by retention_policy, and publishes a signed external anchor (trace_anchor, files under /var/lib/opengrid/anchors) every 15 minutes; the invariants checks verify the chain and flag stale anchors.
