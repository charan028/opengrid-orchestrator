Architecture

OpenGrid runs as six orchestrator processes (og-* systemd units grouped by opengrid.target) that share one PostgreSQL database (schema og) and one Mosquitto MQTT broker. Apache sits in front and handles authentication. The integration simulators (og-sim-*, grouped by ogsim.target) play the part of the hub fleet, SCADA, the market data sources, the utility EMS and the customer operators. Everything below reflects release r3.4.3 (r3.4 plus the r3.4.1, r3.4.2 and r3.4.3 changes).

The nightly database backup is a cron job (deploy/cron/opengrid, 03:00). An optional warm-standby unit and per-process watchdog drop-ins are in deploy/ha/. Since r3.4.1 deploy.sh creates the runtime directories and, after a restart, checks every og-* unit and fresh heartbeats, rolling back on failure.

Kubernetes (alternative deploy, r3.4.1). deploy/k8s/install.sh installs the same solution on a new cluster in phases (prerequisite checks, images, namespace, secrets, deploy, verify; --dry-run, idempotent re-runs, uninstall). One orchestrator image runs every og-* role, a second the simulators; a Helm chart (0.1.0, appVersion r3.4.1) holds Postgres, Mosquitto with the production ACL, migrate and seed Jobs (create_schema.sh, bootstrap phase e), singleton Deployments, og-api behind an Apache sidecar and the lifecycle CronJob (suspended). It reuses the base-server scripts. It has been validated offline only, never on a real cluster, and some r3.4.2/r3.4.3 additions (for example og-sim-utility) have no Kubernetes objects yet; see deploy/k8s/README.md. Production stays on the base server.

The D-34 grid link is off in the shipped config ([grid_link].enabled = false). og-engine calls opengrid.integrations.grid_link.runner.start_grid_link at startup; it opens the DNP3 listener only when [grid_link].enabled and at least one utility's own enabled are true (deploy/scripts/grid_link_enable_loopback.sh does that for AUSTIN_ENERGY on localhost). A link failure never stops the engine. See Grid link.

Since r3.4 deploy.sh reinstalls the release's unit files on every deploy, including og-lifecycle.service and og-lifecycle.timer, but never enables a timer: enable og-lifecycle.timer by hand after an I/O check (see Data lifecycle). The AS-deployment poller (opengrid.contracts.as_deployment_poll) runs in og-feeds from [feeds.ercot_as_poll], which ships with enabled = false. Until the release manager enables it, AS deployments come only from the API (see ERCOT AS deployment instructions).

Functional flow

  1. Market data. og-feeds polls the ERCOT public API, EIA v2 and NWS (or og-sim-market in development) on each source's own schedule and writes normalized points to feed_obs with a quality flag (GOOD, ESTIMATED, STALE, and since r3.4.1 EXTREME_UNCORROBORATED for a price outside the normal band until a second posting corroborates it). feed_status tracks failures and the breaker. A feed that goes STALE makes the health evaluator set the NO_NEW_COMMITMENTS degraded mode; intake is then skipped.
  2. Forecast. Inside the same og-feeds loop, opengrid.forecast recomputes quantile forecasts (p10, p50, p90, firm fitness) every 15 minutes into forecast.
  3. Selector gate. og-engine's gate scheduler starts gates on the 15-minute boundary (a day-ahead plan once a day, intraday otherwise), on admission of a new opportunity, and on a due renomination point. Gates run as one background task so they never hold the 2 s tick. The model is solved with HiGHS; if it is infeasible, times out or fails validation, the rule fallback F2 is used. Selected candidates go through selector.commit: obligations move OFFERED to SELECTED to COMMITTED and the ledger writes reservation and commitment together (K2 one buyer per capacity, K13 commitment lock). Results are kept in plan, plan_value, plan_energy_value and plan_shadow_obligation.
  4. Allocator. Every 2 s og-engine starts the obligation lifecycle step in the background and runs allocator.run_cycle: utility L2 instructions cap capability first, energy is capped over the lease duration, tiers are served in strict order, each obligation is water-filled across eligible hubs, and remaining headroom may follow price. Grants are written to grant. For each bank the engine writes the trace pre-image first, then inserts command_batch, then sends NOTIFY og_command_batch. If the guardian heartbeat is missing, the engine holds and proposes nothing; the whole propose phase is bounded at 2 s (r3.4.3), and banks cut off are reported as timed out. Called capacity holds (an AS deployment or a utility toll call, from any origin through opengrid.calls) are discharged up to the call's requested kW.
  5. Guardian. og-guardian evaluates each new batch against its own reads (G-20 clock check first, then the other G-checks independently). PASS signs the batch with Ed25519 and renews hub leases; VETOED, PARTLY_VETOED and TIMEOUT are holds, never a step to zero for firm obligations (K7). The engine re-solves without vetoed hubs. Sustained vetoes move a bank or zone to CONSERVATIVE in scope_posture.
  6. Hubs. The signed batch goes out on cmd/<bank>/batch and leases on lease/<hub> (retained). Each hub verifies the signature and the epoch, seq and lease freshness, then applies the setpoint or keeps holding the last signed one when the lease expires.
  7. Telemetry. Hubs publish tel/... every 10 s ([fleet].telemetry_interval_s) and an ack/<hub> per command. og-engine updates the in-memory fleet twin and a separate periodic task flushes hub_state, telemetry and command_ack. The guardian keeps its own telemetry cache and does not trust the engine's copy. A hub is stale after [health].hub_stale_s (25 s) and offline after hub_offline_s (60 s); the fleet twin, the health rules and the console use the same two values. The next allocator cycle reads this state.
  8. Settle. Every 60 s og-settle meters pending obligation intervals from telemetry (meter_interval), scores performance, writes versioned invoice_line rows and pnl (including the delivery charge where configured; ERCOT_AS capacity at the cleared MCPC), and closes FULFILLED or SHORTFALL obligations to SETTLED.
  9. Delivery verification. Every 15 s og-settle's delivery job (D-38, r3.4.3) builds an og.delivery_record for each running or just-ended discharge call (utility call, AS deployment, manual discharge target) from 30 s buckets of commanded (signed) and delivered (telemetry) kW, raises and clears the ALR-DELIVERY-* alerts, flags a measured shortfall AT_RISK and traces the final PASS, PARTIAL or FAIL result. The call status the utility reads comes from it. See Delivery verification.
  10. Safe-stop path. An operator stop is two-step: POST /og/api/safestop then /confirm; og-api passes both to og-safestop through NOTIFY og_safestop_request, and a single message can never stop the fleet. Utility BLOCK or ESTOP instructions trigger a bank stop directly. og-safestop writes stop_event and stop_outbox in one transaction, then publishes the retained stop/<scope>/<id> message. Release needs two operators and a guardian signature. See Safe stop.
  11. Trace journal. Every decision is appended to trace as a per-stream hash chain and mirrored to the local journal file. og-settle writes checkpoints, prunes by retention_policy, and publishes a signed external anchor (trace_anchor, files under /var/lib/opengrid/anchors) every 15 minutes; the invariants checks verify the chain and flag stale anchors.
