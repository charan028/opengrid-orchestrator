| D-38 | 2026-09-26 | **Delivery is verified from measured telemetry, never from grants (r3.4.3).** A grant is planned power: a grant the guardian vetoes still counts as granted. The rules are one pure module, `opengrid.core.delivery`; og-settle's delivery job (`opengrid.delivery`, `[delivery]`, enabled by default) applies them. **What is verified:** every discharge call: a utility toll call or an ERCOT AS deployment, whatever its origin (call id = the deployment id), and an operator manual discharge target (call id = its MANUAL_TARGET trace id). **Measurement:** every 15 s (`interval_s`) the job evaluates each running or just-ended call in 30 s buckets aligned to the call's start (`bucket_s`), reading a bucket 30 s after it ends (`telemetry_lag_s`), until the call has a final record (at most 1 h after it ended, `lookback_s`). Delivered kW comes from hub telemetry `p_kw`: an obligation's share of each bank's measured discharge, by its share of the bank's granted kW (the settlement rule, `attributed_discharge_kw`), or a manual target's own hubs. Commanded kW is the call's items in guardian-signed (PASS) batches. A bucket without telemetry is unmeasured (DATA_STALE), never 0. **Tolerance and sustain:** delivered must reach **95% of the committed kW** (`target_frac`) **within the product's ramp time** (`[delivery.ramp_time_s]`, by contract variant: ECRS, RRS and TOLLING 600 s, REGUP and REGDN 300 s, NSPIN 1800 s, MANUAL 120 s, anything else 600 s), else RAMP_TOO_SLOW. From the earlier of reaching it and that deadline, **at least 95% of the measured buckets** (`sustain_pass_pct`) must stay at or above it, else SUSTAIN_BELOW_TARGET. Also: DATA_STALE when over 10% of buckets are unmeasured, NO_DELIVERY at most 5% of committed on average, ENERGY_SHORT under 50% of the committed kWh, VETOED when at least half the proposed command cycles were vetoed, STOPPED when the call was ended early. **Result:** IN_PROGRESS while the call runs; then FAIL (all stale, NO_DELIVERY or ENERGY_SHORT), PARTIAL (any other reason) or PASS. It is kept with its per-bucket series in `og.delivery_record` (migration 0050), and the final record is traced (stream `delivery`, `DELIVERY_VERIFICATION`). **Meter:** on banks with an independent meter (a SUBSTATION asset's bank, plus `meter_bank_ids`), the change of SCADA `REAL_POWER_KW` is compared with the battery telemetry's, both from pre-call baselines (`baseline_s`, 120 s): UNCORROBORATED when the call's energy differs by over 10% (`meter_tolerance_frac`, floored at 25 kW), NO_METER, METER_STALE under 50% coverage. Manual targets are never metered. **Alerts** (critical, raised and cleared only by the delivery job): while a call runs, `ALR-DELIVERY-RAMP-LATE` (past its ramp time, never at target), `ALR-DELIVERY-SHORTFALL` (below target for 60 s, `shortfall_alert_s`) and `ALR-DELIVERY-NONE` (signed commands, no measurable delivery for 60 s, `none_alert_s`); on an UNCORROBORATED final record `ALR-DELIVERY-METER-MISMATCH` (final r3.4.3: cleared once the same meter agrees again on a later call, or by an operator with a reason via `POST /og/api/delivery/records/{call_id}/meter-mismatch/clear` (traced)). A live SHORTFALL or NONE flags the obligation AT_RISK (`R-DELIVERY-MEASURED-SHORTFALL`), cleared on recovery or at the call's end. **Where it shows:** the operator API (`GET /og/api/delivery/records`, `/records/{call_id}`, `/summary`); the call status the utility customer API and the grid link read, now from MEASURED delivered kW: RAMPING below and DELIVERING at or above `[dispatch.calls].ramping_fraction` (0.9) of the call's target, ACTIVE while unmeasured or stale, with `delivered_kw`/`delivered_kwh`, `delivery_measured` true once the job has evaluated a bucket of the call, and `delivery_state` the record's result. Grid-link AI 5 (CALL_DELIVERED_KW) serves that measured kW, COMM_LOST while unmeasured or stale. It only observes: a measured shortfall never changes dispatch or stops a firm obligation (K7, D-17), and settlement still bills measured energy. r3.4.1 and r3.4.2 ran none of this (`delivery_measured` was always false; D-33 had planned it for r3.4.2). The e2e suites check the same calls from telemetry and against `og.delivery_record` (`tests-e2e/functional/delivery_check.py`). **Final call rules (r3.4.3):** `delivery_measured` is true only once a measured delivered value (or a final verdict) exists, so a running call whose first buckets had no telemetry stays ACTIVE / UNMEASURED; the planned `granted_kw` / `granted_kwh` / `granted_description` fields are kept for one release, deprecated (removed in r3.5; use `delivered_*`); a utility may read every call on its own toll but cancel or shorten only a call it issued itself (else 403 `R-CALL-NOT-ISSUER`, traced AUTHZ_DENY; an operator may end any call); cancelling or shortening a call that has already ended answers 409 `R-CALL-ALREADY-ENDED`. | DELIVERY-VERIFY work package (`5826c77` core; `2251392` job, alerts, API and call status; `54079b9` grid link), planned in D-33 | `opengrid.core.delivery`, `opengrid.delivery`, `opengrid.health.delivery_rules`, migration 0050, `[delivery]`, `opengrid.calls` status, customer API status, integrations/grid-link.md (AI 5), operator guide 8.1, `tests-e2e/functional` |
