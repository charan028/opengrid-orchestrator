# 05 — Reviewer claims verification (primary-source fact-check)

Status: complete · Accessed: 2026-09-25 (all sources) · Scope: 15 regulatory and technical claims raised in the adversarial reviews (GRD-*, JDG-* IDs)

Rule applied throughout: a reviewer claim is a hypothesis. It is accepted only if the primary text says it. Where the text says something narrower, broader or different, the verdict states this and gives the exact wording.

---

## 1. Method

**Verdict scale.** *Confirmed*: the primary text says it. *Partly confirmed*: some elements are in the text; others are missing, narrower or different, and the row says which. *Not confirmed*: no primary support found. *Contradicted*: the primary text says otherwise. *Could not access*: the primary text could not be read.

**The WebFetch hallucination rule.** WebFetch was used only to download files. Its summaries are not evidence. In an earlier pass it invented a "50 MW, 20/15/15 MW" ADER cap that the ERCOT PDF does not contain. For that reason:
- **Binary files (PDF, DOCX, XLSX)** were saved locally and parsed with stdlib-only scripts in the session scratchpad (`pdftext.py`, `pdfpos.py`, `docx2txt.py`, `xlsxdump.py`). The PDF scripts pull text from the content streams. The DOCX script reads `word/document.xml` and marks tracked changes as `{+ins+}` / `[-del-]`. The XLSX script reads the shared strings and the sheet XML. All quotes from these files were taken from the local text, not from a WebFetch answer.
- **The slide 8 table** in the ERCOT Load Resource deck could not be rendered (no PDF renderer is installed, and none was installed). Its columns were rebuilt from the text coordinates in the PDF content stream, for example the CLR header at x=364 and the values at x=351 to 354.
- **HTML pages** (statutes, Texas Register, NPRR pages, press releases, GitHub) were fetched with prompts that ask for verbatim text only. A number or a legal phrase from HTML was accepted only when a second fetch, or a second independent source, returned the same text. Items read only once are marked "(1 read)".

**How current the rules are.** ERCOT Nodal Protocols were read as published: Section 2 (2026-08-01), Section 6 (2026-08-28) and Section 8 (2026-08-01). Language that is approved but not yet implemented (ERCOT's "gray box" text) is reported as *pending*.

**Quotation policy.** Verbatim quotes of 25 words or fewer are taken only from binding rules and public records: statutes, the enrolled bill, the Texas Register proposal, the ERCOT Protocols, the ADER Governing Document and the baseline document it incorporates, ERCOT market notices and NPRR reports, the PJM OATT and the City of Austin RCA. Copyrighted material that is not a rule (ERCOT training decks, the CRA report, Modo, press releases, news, vendor READMEs, licences and code) is paraphrased with a pinpoint location. Identifiers (code names, table labels, cell values) are given exactly, in code format. Where no rule text exists, one short deck quote is used (claim 14). Page numbers given for GD 3.3 are the section start pages from its table of contents. Slide numbers are the deck's own page numbers.

**Not accessible.**
- statutes.capitol.texas.gov returned only a navigation shell. The enrolled SB 231 PDF and FindLaw were used instead.
- The texas.public.law page for §39.918 was found to be **stale**: it lacks the SB 231 amendments.
- law.justia.com returned HTTP 403.
- IEEE 1815-2012 is paywalled and was not read. Claim 13 therefore rests on two independent vendor implementations.

## 3. Consequences for the specification

Each item gives the design value or rule to adopt and names the evidence. Items marked **[assumption]** are not settled by primary text and must stay visible as assumptions.

1. **ALR-type ADER control loop (GRD-001).**
   - Model the ALR-ADER as a SCED-dispatched CLR. Its setpoint is the ERCOT UDSP (base ramp plus regulation), received every 4 s. Online Non-Spin and ECRS carry **no separate deployment message**: ECRS energy is inside the base point (§6.5.7.6.2.4(6)), and CLRs are absent from the XML Non-Spin list (§6.5.7.6.2.3(7)).
   - Measure compliance on UDSP: CLREDP in §8 and the Set Point Deviation Charge in §6.6.5.1. Use the new term; GD 3.3 §5.g still says "Base Point Deviation".
   - Keep a manual/XML instruction handler for emergencies. ECRS energy "may also be manually deployed" under §6.5.9 (§6.5.7.6.2.4(3)), and §6.5.7.6.2.4(8) speaks of XML for "Load Resources providing ECRS" generically.
   - Resource-level telemetry every **2 s** (GD §5.d, same source).

2. **AS capability telemetry is an offer (GRD-002).**
   - Every SCED run, ERCOT creates a proxy AS offer for every qualified Resource. It spans all products the Resource is qualified for; its MW equals MPC for Load Resources; and awards are capped by telemetered AS capability. No AS responsibility is required.
   - The proxy price is at least min($2,000/MW-h, ASDC at 95% of the AS Plan) − $0.01. It rarely clears, but it clears in scarcity.
   - Rule for the orchestrator:
     - Telemetered per-product capability must equal the MW the operator accepts being awarded. It should also stay within the Non-Spin/ECRS MW in the ERCOT-signed submission, which caps an ADER's AS offers and responsibilities (GD §5.d, last paragraph). Applying that cap to telemetry is a design rule, not a quoted requirement.
     - Always submit RT AS offers and an Energy Bid Curve that cover all telemetered capability. Without an energy bid, ERCOT bids the CLR's LPC-to-MPC range at VOLL (§6.5.7.3(9)).
     - An awarded AS obligation survives OUTL (§6.5.7.3(10)).

3. **NCLR-type ADER lane (GRD-017).**
   - It is not SCED-*dispatched*, but SCED still *awards* AS each interval. Ingest the AS award points over ICCP.
   - Deploy only on the XML instruction, and hold the response until recall (§8.1.1.4.3(3)(d)).
   - Evaluate performance against the GD's MBMA baseline: the kWh of the full 15-min interval before the instruction. Also compute the generic NCLR 5-min telemetry baseline (§8.1.1.4.3(3)(e)). Which one ERCOT applies in practice must be confirmed with ERCOT **[assumption]**.
   - Target band: at least 95% and at most 150% of the instruction (Non-Spin: from 30 min after the instruction; ECRS: within 10 min).
   - Counted failure: below 95%. **Two failures in any rolling 365 days means disqualification**, with reapplication after 6 months and a corrective action plan (§8.1.1.4.3(5), §8.1.1.1(9)). Size headroom so that a single weak event cannot become a second strike.

4. **Aggregation eligibility and data (GRD-016/042).**
   - Enforce one Load Zone, one LSE and one DSP per ALR-ADER.
   - For an NCLR-ADER, enforce a signed LSE Acknowledgment for every LSE.
   - Premises of 100 kW or less must be associated with the submitting LSE in both models. Third-party aggregation of residential premises across LSEs is not available in Phase 3.3.
   - Export at a premise is negative load in the LSE QSE's settlement; the LSE QSE acknowledges the impact.
   - Data retention requirement: premise- and/or device-level net-MW time series, **battery SOC time series**, and static per-premise allocation factors, exportable to ERCOT on request.

5. **PURA §39.918 (GRD-005).**
   - Remove any §39.918 revenue or partner lane for the fleet. Such facilities must be isolated from the bulk system, kept out of ERCOT pricing and reliability models, and generate no energy or AS sales. For leases from 2025-06-20 they must also be mobile, movable in under 12 h, and 5 MW or less.
   - The section does not reach co-ops or municipally owned utilities.
   - Update the register: the texas.public.law text is pre-SB 231.

6. **AS durations (GRD-056 / Q7).** Use NPRR1282 values, in force since RTC+B go-live on 2025-12-05.
   - ADER products: **ECRS 1 h, Non-Spin 4 h**. Make Non-Spin a parameter that switches to **2 h** when NPRR1309 (DRRS) is implemented.
   - Non-ADER lanes: Regulation 30 min; RRS 30 min for SCED-dispatchable Resources and 1 h for UFR NCLRs; FFR 15 min. RUC uses 60 min (FFR 15 min).
   - Mark the CRA report's "ECRS 2 h" as superseded wherever the spec cites it.

7. **EEA charging (GRD-004).**
   - The legal duty in §6.5.9.4.2 binds QSEs for **registered ESRs** today, and for SOESSs once NPRR995 is implemented. Exceptions: a current SCED, LFC or manual instruction to charge; active PFR; or co-location behind a POI with non-exporting onsite generation.
   - The residential ADER fleet (Load Resources) is outside the text. Adopt the rule as operator policy: during any EEA, suppress discretionary charging in all non-ERCOT lanes. For an ALR-ADER, follow SCED/UDSP.
   - Record it as a voluntary policy, not a compliance item.

8. **Dual-use co-op fleets (GRD-024 / Q6).**
   - The GVEC evidence shows one fleet that is qualified for the ADER pilot and dispatched by the utility to cut transmission and energy costs. The 4CP and arbitrage use is reported for 2025, before qualification.
   - Design the lane arbiter to hold both on the same assets, with the ERCOT award as a hard constraint.
   - Keep "concurrent peak/price dispatch after qualification" as an **[assumption]** to validate with the utility.

9. **Utility tolling (GRD-015).**
   - For AE-type contracts, reserve the contracted MW (40 MW, about 1.5 h) under the utility's charge/discharge control as an external, non-optimisable schedule, with an 18-month ramp to 40 MW.
   - Derived price ceiling: at most $8.50/kW-month ($4.08M ÷ 12 ÷ 40,000 kW). This is an upper bound that assumes the full 40 MW all year.

10. **TDU storage contracts (GRD-048).**
    - Cap each TDU's contracted MW at its load-ratio share of the 100 MW statewide limit.
    - Preconditions: prior PUCT authorization; competitive bidding (RFP); cheaper than traditional distribution facilities.
    - The contracted MW is discharged for the contract only on TDU direction. The PGC may sell into ERCOT only while it honours the reservation.
    - Label §25.58 as a proposed rule (Project 59523), not adopted as of this check.

11. **ComEd PLC (GRD-049).**
    - Model PLC(Y+1, Jun–May) = the average of the customer's load at PJM's 5 summer peaks, **plus PJM Load Drop Estimates**, plus losses, with a ComEd-peak adjustment. A delivery-class average may replace it "for certain situations".
    - Consequence: reductions counted as PJM demand-response load drops are added back; only non-DR metered reduction lowers the PLC (inference from the definitions).
    - Verify per-customer treatment with ComEd before booking PLC savings **[assumption]**.

12. **DNP3 stack (JDG-008).**
    - stepfunc has no first-party Python binding. Use it through the C API/FFI, or use Java or .NET.
    - Production use requires a commercial licence; the public licence is non-production only.
    - For VOLTTRON `dnp3-python`, do not specify DNP3-over-TLS unless the build is customised (`DNP3_TLS=ON` plus OpenSSL) and maintained in-house. Otherwise terminate TLS outside the process (stunnel, VPN).

13. **IIN handling (GRD-052).**
    - IIN1 bit 7 (0x80), device restart: integrity poll, then write to clear IIN1.7.
    - IIN2 bit 3 (0x08), event buffer overflow: integrity poll, and flag the interval as a data gap for settlement evidence.
    - The bit positions are verified. The response actions are standard DNP3 master behaviour, not checked against IEEE 1815.

14. **UDSP following (GRD-022/013).**
    - Setpoint cadence is 4 s (protocol).
    - Base-ramp shape: configurable, default 4-min linear to the new base point (ERCOT training value; the Protocols give no duration).
    - Dispatch envelope per SCED interval: HDL/LDL = telemetered power ± 5 × normal ramp rate, clipped to MPC/LPC. Telemetry of MPC, LPC and ramp rates is therefore a control input and must be conservative and accurate.

15. **ADER limits and consent (JDG-017).**
    - Replace 160/80/80 MW with **500 MW energy / 100 MW Non-Spin / 100 MW ECRS**, with no QSE above 90% of each (GD 3.2 / 3.3). ERCOT may raise the limits without Board approval.
    - Plan with **zero ECRS headroom**: 100.0 MW is approved as of 06-01-26, and 97.3 MW was qualified in 2026-08.
    - Consent: DSP Acknowledgment (Appendix B) at start, and a DSP e-mail with an updated DOTA for each premise added. The DSP can rescind with 30 days' notice.
    - LZ_AEN and LZ_CPS have 0 MW approved, so the NOIE's consent as DSP is the gating condition for any ERCOT lane there.

## 4. Corrections to earlier project facts

- **ADER limits.** "160/80/80 MW" and "limits shared by load zone" (Phase 3 board item, June 2025) are superseded by 500/100/100 MW with a 90% per-QSE cap (GD 3.2 of 2026-02-27 and GD 3.3). The 06-01-26 tracking sheet has no per-zone limits.
- **ECRS duration.** "ECRS 2 h for Load Resources" (CRA report, April 2026) conflicts with the Protocols. It has been **1 h** since NPRR1282 took effect on 2025-12-05. Non-Spin is 4 h, pending 2 h under NPRR1309.
- **NCLR performance.** "Two failures can suspend" (ERCOT LR training deck) understates the rule. The Protocols say two failures in a rolling 365 days "shall result in disqualification".
- **PURA §39.918.** The cited texas.public.law text omits SB 231, which has been in effect since 2025-06-20.
- **GRD-048 statute.** The contract rules are in PURA **§35.153**. §35.152 only classifies storage as a generation asset.
